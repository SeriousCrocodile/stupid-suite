# Stupid Dispatch — Field Logistics & QR Fulfillment

---

## 1. Core Purpose
`Stupid Dispatch` empowers trusted partners, friends, or family members to fulfill physical orders with zero access to Shopify or Etsy admin credentials.

---

## 2. Key Modules
- **QR Label Generator:** Prints thermal barcode/QR labels for individual ziplock bags and storage bin organizers.
- **Morning Packing List:** Clean, picture-based packing checklist for fulfillment helpers (e.g. at the Lille storage hub) with item photo, size, and shelf coordinate.
- **Smartphone Camera Verification:** Scans the picked item's QR code to guarantee the correct piece and size are placed in the parcel.
- **1-Tap Shipped:** Automatically updates tracking numbers across Shopify and Etsy upon scan confirmation.
