# Stupid Money — Receipt Vision OCR & Fiscal Intelligence

---

## 1. Core Purpose
`Stupid Money` delivers effortless financial bookkeeping and real net profit visibility for independent makers.

---

## 2. Key Modules
- **Receipt & Invoice OCR Ingestion:** Upload receipts via web or dropzone; Gemini Vision extracts merchant, date, VAT, total amount, and item details.
- **Fiscal Auto-Categorization:** Tags expenses into proper accounting buckets (Raw Materials, Tools & Equipment, Shipping & Packaging, Marketing).
- **Monthly & Annual P&L Reports:** Clean financial statements ready for certified public accountants and tax filings.
- **AI Financial Copilot:** Natural language Q&A regarding cashflow, platform fees, and material cost fluctuations over time.
