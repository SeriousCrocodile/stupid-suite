# Stupid Content — Social Media & Wholesale Lookbooks

---

## 1. Core Purpose
`Stupid Content` converts raw product renders and specs into public engagement channels and wholesale sales collateral.

---

## 2. Key Modules
- **Social Format Exporter:** Automatically frames and scales 3x3 crops into Instagram carousels (`4:5`), Reels/Stories (`9:16`), and TikTok covers.
- **Newsletter Composer:** Markdown email generator combining hero visuals, product details, and copy presets.
- **B2B Line Sheet & Lookbook Compiler:** Generates high-resolution wholesale PDF catalogs with wholesale pricing, minimum order quantities (MOQ), and material breakdown for boutique buyers.
