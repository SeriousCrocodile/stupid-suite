# Stupid Stock — Physical Inventory & Production Margins

---

## 1. Core Purpose
`Stupid Stock` answers three fundamental questions for the artisan:
1. What items and raw materials exist physically right now?
2. Where are they located (e.g., France/Lille hub vs. Bali workshop vs. Bangkok atelier)?
3. What is our exact unit cost of production and true net margin after marketplace transaction fees?

---

## 2. Key UX Capabilities
- **High-Density Spreadsheet View:** Blazing fast inline keyboard navigation (`Tab`, `Enter`, arrows) with instant cell editing.
- **3-Tier Inventory Segregation:**
  - `B2C Finished Goods:` Ready to ship for direct retail orders.
  - `B2B / Consignment:` Locked in wholesale consignment with retail partners.
  - `Raw Materials:` Grams of 925 silver, brass casting ingots, leather hides, hardware.
- **Dynamic Fee Adjustment Simulator:** Toggle simulated fees (Shopify 2.9% + €0.30, Etsy 6.5% + transaction fees) to compute net profitability.
- **1-Click Bridge to Stupid Shoot:** Filter items lacking photos and export a batch job directly to `Stupid Shoot`.
